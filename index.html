How to play

👆 Tap to Fly: Tap anywhere on the screen to gently float up. Release to glide down.
✨ Phototaxis: Collect golden glowing fireflies. Higher combos multiply your score!
🌲 Avoid Obstacles: Dodge the glowing pillars in the dark night.
🛡 Shield: Protects your lantern flame from one collision.
🧲 Light Radius: Magnetically pulls nearby fireflies to your light.
⬆ Reach higher scores to unlock more levels and faster flights!
